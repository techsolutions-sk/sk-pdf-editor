# SK PDF Editor - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

SK PDF Editor works entirely on your own device. Your PDF files, images, signatures and text are opened, edited and converted inside the app on your computer. They are never uploaded to us or to any server.

- We do not collect, store or share any personal information.
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
- The app does not require an account.

OCR feature: the first time you use OCR, the app downloads the free open-source OCR engine (Tesseract.js) and its language data from a public content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Only these program files are downloaded; your documents are not sent anywhere. OCR then runs on your device.
